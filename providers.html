How to get started

Start with the clients you already serve.

waitAround connects you with returning clients — not through a marketplace, but through the relationship you already have.

1

Share your waiting-room QR

Keep it at your station or show it directly from your phone. No printing required to get started.

2

Ask clients to scan during a visit

They connect with your waiting room through waitAround Clients when they're at the shop.

3

Let them join remotely next time

Returning clients can request a place in your same-day line without calling or texting first.

The barber experience

You control how the line moves.

Every decision stays with you. waitAround gives you a clearer view of who is waiting — nothing moves without your action.

View the active line

See who is waiting, their position, and when they joined.

Notify next

Alert the next client in line when you're ready for them.

Start and finish service

Mark when a client is in the chair and when you're done.

Handle no-shows

Mark clients who aren't present when called and move the line forward.

Why it helps

A clearer walk-in process for you and your clients.

Fewer "how many ahead?" questions

Clients can check their position in the app instead of calling or stopping by to ask.

Clients don't have to sit inside

Connected clients can wait nearby and come in as their turn gets closer.

One consistent way to connect

One QR code gives returning clients a repeatable way to join your same-day line.

Less back-and-forth over texts

Reduce the number of individual messages about line status throughout the day.

Your walk-in model

Keep walk-ins.
Change the waiting.

waitAround supports your existing walk-in model rather than replacing it with scheduled appointments. Your clients still get the flexibility of a same-day walk-in. You get a more organized way to manage who is waiting and who comes next.
